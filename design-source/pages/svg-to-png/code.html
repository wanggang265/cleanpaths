Why convert SVG to PNG?

SVG is the right format for icons, logos and illustrations — until you need to put one somewhere that doesn't speak vector. Social platforms, slide decks, Word documents, most email clients and plenty of marketplaces want a plain bitmap. PNG is the bitmap that treats vector art well: sharp edges, a transparent background, and no compression smearing. This converter renders your SVG to PNG right in your browser — no upload, and the preview shows exactly what you'll get.

The transparent background stays transparent

Most SVG artwork floats on nothing — that's what makes it useful. Converted carelessly, or screenshotted, it comes back flattened onto white or black. PNG supports a full alpha channel, so the conversion keeps your artwork on transparency: ready to drop over any color, slide background or product photo without a box around it. JPG can't do this — if anyone suggests converting your SVG to JPG for the web, point them to our SVG to JPG page, which explains exactly what gets traded away.

This page goes vector to pixels. There's a page for the other direction.

Converting SVG to PNG rasterizes your artwork — the result looks identical but no longer scales. If what you actually need is the opposite — a bitmap, like a screenshot or a photo of a logo, turned into clean editable vector paths — that's a different tool: our Screenshot to SVG converter traces bitmaps into layered vectors, and other CleanPaths converters can take it further to DXF for cutting machines. Rasterizing down is the easy direction; tracing back up is the hard problem, and it has its own pages.

Vectors have no pixels — so you pick the size

An SVG is a set of drawing instructions, not a grid of dots, which means converting it to PNG requires a decision no vector file ever makes for you: how many pixels wide should the result be? Render small and you get a fast, lightweight image for the web. Render large and you get detail that survives printing and zooming. The exact size and resolution options live in the tool above — this copy doesn't lock numbers the implementation may refine; the preview always shows what the current settings produce before you download.

FAQ
What resolution will the PNG be?
That's your choice, not the file's — a vector has no fixed pixel size, so the tool offers output size options when you convert. As a rule of thumb: web avatars and thumbnails want small, print and zoomed detail want large. The preview shows exactly what the current choice produces before you download.
Will the PNG have a transparent background?
Yes. If your SVG artwork has no background of its own, the alpha channel carries over — PNG supports full transparency. That's the main reason to pick PNG over JPG for logos and icons.
Why does my SVG look different in some apps?
SVG can reference fonts and effects that different viewers render differently — the file is an instruction set, and every app interprets it its own way. Converting to PNG freezes exactly what you see in the preview into pixels, which is one practical reason to convert before sharing the image.
Do you upload or store my files?
No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser — there is no upload step. We never receive, see, or store your images or the files you generate. Close the tab and they're gone; we never had them. This is how the tool is built, not a marketing claim. Details in our Privacy Policy.
