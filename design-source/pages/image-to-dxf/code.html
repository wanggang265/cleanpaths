The pipeline

One page for every image, one standard for the output

FAQ
Which image formats can I convert here?
The upload box on this page lists exactly what it accepts — that list is the answer, and the file never leaves your device for you to find out. As a rule: if it's a common image format, drop it in and the preview shows what the converter sees. Photos convert too, but expect a stylized, posterized result rather than cut-ready output.
How is this different from the PNG to DXF and JPG to DXF converters?
Same engine, same output standard — the dedicated pages are tuned and written around one format each. If you know your format, the PNG to DXF or JPG to DXF page speaks directly to it. If you have a mix of images, or you're just not sure what you're holding, this page is the front door: it takes the common formats and gives them all the same cleanup, tracing and DXF export.
Will the DXF work with my cutter, laser or CNC?
The output is all-POLYLINE DXF with closed, color-separated paths — the shape of file cut software expects. Cricut Design Space imports SVG, so grab the SVG download for Cricut workflows. LightBurn, AutoCAD and most CNC CAM speak DXF directly; the default R2000 export embeds units, so the design opens at the size you drew it. Always run a test cut before production.
Do you upload or store my images?
No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser — there is no upload step. We never receive, see, or store your images or the files you generate. Close the tab and they're gone; we never had them. This is how the tool is built, not a marketing claim. Details in our Privacy Policy.
