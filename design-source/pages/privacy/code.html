Privacy Policy

The one-line version: your files never leave your device — conversion happens in your browser, and there is no upload step. The details below are written to be read, not hidden.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Who we are

CleanPaths ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Gang Wang (individual, operating as CleanPaths), located at China. You can reach us at privacy@cleanpaths.io.

CleanPaths is a browser-based tool that converts images (PNG, JPG, SVG, screenshots) into cut-ready DXF/SVG files for makers, Cricut sellers, and embroidery machine owners.

2. The short version — your files never leave your device

All file conversion happens entirely inside your browser. Your images and the files you generate are processed locally using WebAssembly (WASM) and, for optional AI upscaling, your device's WebGPU. We do not receive, store, see, or transmit your files. When you close the tab, your files are gone — we never had them.

This is not a marketing claim; it is how the tool is built. There is no upload step. (Free tier: fully local. Pro tier: also fully local — Pro unlocks features via a license key, not by moving processing to a server.)

4. Payment processing (Merchant of Record)

Purchases are handled by our Merchant of Record Creem, which acts as the seller of record and an independent controller of your payment data. They process your card, billing details, and applicable taxes (VAT/GST/sales tax) under their own privacy policy. We recommend you read it: Creem Privacy Policy.

We receive from them only what we need to deliver your purchase: your email address, order ID, license key, and country. We never receive, process, or store your card number.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy — the short version is: one strictly-necessary browser storage entry (your free-tier usage counter and license cache) and cookieless aggregate analytics. No ad networks, no retargeting, no selling data. Ever.

6. Testimonials and "Show Your Work" (planned, v1.1)

We may feature user testimonials and photos of finished work on the site. We will only do this with your explicit written consent (obtained by email reply), you can withdraw it at any time by emailing privacy@cleanpaths.io, and we will remove the content within 14 days of withdrawal. We will never lift your feedback text or images without asking first.

8. International data transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required (EEA/UK/Brazil), transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses via our processors' data processing agreements.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority:

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale/sharing. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you have the rights listed above; our legal bases are contract performance (license delivery), legitimate interest (abuse prevention, product improvement), and consent (testimonials).
  • Brazil (LGPD): you have the rights under Art. 18 of Law 13.709/2018. Our PT-language pages follow the same minimal-collection architecture described here.

To exercise any right, email privacy@cleanpaths.io. We respond within 30 days.

10. Data retention

See the table in §3. In short: feedback emails up to 12 months; purchase/license records for the life of your license plus statutory tax periods; rate-limiting hashes under 24 hours; no file data at all. You can ask us to delete your feedback or personal data at any time (license records may need to be kept for tax/legal reasons — we'll tell you if so).

11. Security

Because your files never reach us, the biggest category of risk simply doesn't exist. For the small amount of data we do hold, we use TLS in transit, access-limited mailboxes, and vendor infrastructure (Cloudflare / MoR) with industry-standard security.

12. Children

CleanPaths is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/Brazil where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their data. With no accounts and no uploads, children's use of the converter itself leaves no data with us.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, note them on this page. This page is part of our copy-freeze set: changes go through review before publishing.
