Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 ｜ Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short answer: we don't use tracking cookies

CleanPaths is built on a no-tracking-cookie architecture. There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing that legally requires one (no ad cookies, no analytics cookies, no cross-site trackers).

What your browser does store

ItemTypePurposeDuration
Free-tier usage counterlocalStorage (not a cookie)Remembers how many free conversions you've used today so the 10/day limit worksResets daily
License cachelocalStorage (not a cookie)Keeps your Pro license active offline after activationUntil you clear site data
UI preferences (if any)localStorageRemembers settings like your preferred DXF versionUntil you clear site data

These are strictly necessary for the Service to function. Under GDPR/ePrivacy (and Brazil's LGPD), strictly-necessary storage does not require consent. None of it is used for advertising, profiling, or tracking across sites.

Analytics without cookies

We currently do not use any third-party analytics service. If we add one in the future, it will be a privacy-focused analytics tool that works without cookies and collects only aggregate, anonymous statistics (page views, approximate region, device type). It will not identify you and will not follow you around the web.

If this ever changes

If we ever introduce any non-essential cookie, we will: (1) update this page first, and (2) show a consent banner before setting it. We don't expect this to happen.

Managing your choices

You can clear or block local storage in your browser settings at any time. Blocking it may degrade the Service (e.g., the free daily counter and Pro offline activation may not work).
